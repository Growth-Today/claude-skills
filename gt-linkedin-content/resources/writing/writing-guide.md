# Growth Today LinkedIn Writing Guide

How we sound and how we format, the credibility moves, the pillars, and the pre-publish checklist. The single reference for what to post and how to write it.

## Content Philosophy: The Growth Today DNA

1. **Show, don't just tell**, walk through exactly how we built it, step by step
2. **Specific numbers always**, have data instead of opinions. Never "many", never "several", always "37" or "12 hours" or "4.7% reply rate"
3. **Building in public**, process transparency, client results, tool stack, team decisions
4. **Practitioner voice**, we've done this, not just read about it. "We built" not "you should consider building"
5. **Authority through value**, readers should be able to replicate what we do. Name drop tools, experts, frameworks. 
6. **Aesthetic multiply reach**, image posts get 2.3x reactions vs text-only. Always recommend a visual.

## Content Pillars (Rotate These)

General guidance for the content mix. Match a request to a pillar, then pick a formula and a funnel stage. Fill in company-specific numbers supplied by the user, never invent or hardcode them.

| Pillar | Weight | Funnel | Examples |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| **AI Agents & Automation** | 25% | ToF + MoF | Marketing automation, sales automation, ops delivery automation, agentic GTM workflows, not just Claude Code, but all AI-powered process automation |
| **AI / Tech Trends** | 15% | ToF | "What this new thing means for your GTM motion", position yourself as the translator between AI news and B2B operators |
| **Outbound Tactics** | 20% | MoF + BoF | Cold email teardowns, sequence structures, deliverability guides, reply rate benchmarks, signal-based trigger lists |
| **ICP & Signal-Based GTM** | 15% | MoF | TAM building, persona frameworks, intent signal stacks, Clay workflows, waterfall enrichment, a core signal-based GTM expertise area |
| **Tech Stack & Tool Reveals** | 10% | MoF | "Every tool we use to run our agency", head-to-head comparisons, "X just changed the game" |
| **LinkedIn Growth & Content** | 10% | MoF | Campaign breakdowns, content strategy reveals, posting system, also positions LinkedIn content as a service |
| **Agency Building & Lessons/Frameworks** | 5% | MoF + BoF | Building in public (process, team, client results) + breaking down frameworks from big GTM leaders/research with your own commentary |

Match each pillar to a formula and funnel stage, and keep the topics current by watching what is in demand in your market right now. Lead with fresh angles before they saturate, and never post a trending take you cannot back with a real number or a real build.

## Weekly Plan

Build a week like this using the pillars above:

- **Monday:** Pillar 3 or 4 (MoF, an outbound tactic or an ICP system reveal).
- **Tuesday:** Pillar 1 (MoF, an AI agent or automation build showcase).
- **Wednesday:** Pillar 4 or 5 (MoF, an ICP teardown or a tool comparison).
- **Thursday:** Pillar 3 (BoF, a resource drop with a comment-gate, if a real asset is ready).
- **Friday:** Pillar 2 (ToF, an AI trend explainer with a GTM angle).
- **Weekend (optional):** only worth it once your audience is large enough for weekend reach (see the timing note in algorithm.md). Save it for the highest-effort evergreen, a system reveal or a journey post.

Always match the topic to the person posting. If you run more than one voice (for example Brigi and Jani), give each the pillars that fit their remit.

## Writing Voice & Tone

### DO:

- **Keep it conversational and first-person** ("I", "We")
- **Start with the result or bold claim**, never context first
- **Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3** for any process or walkthrough
- **Arrow lines (↳)** for sub-points under each step, the GTM LinkedIn signature format
- **Specific numbers always**: "657 leads", "12 hours/week", "4.7% reply rate", "40 meetings in 6 weeks"
- **"Here's what we did / Here's the exact system / Here's the workflow"**, standard reveal bridge
- **P.S. section**, mandatory at the end of every post (see P.S. rules below)
- **Bold unicode** for 1-3 key phrases per post (+124% reach)
- **Generous whitespace**, visual break every 2-3 lines, no walls of text (+212% reactions)

### DON'T:

- **Start with an observation hook**, "We ran...", "Last week...", "I was thinking...", "I noticed...", "Recently...", these are setup sentences, not hooks
- Start with context, preamble, or "I've been thinking about..."
- Use vague quantifiers: "many", "several", "a lot", "tons of"
- Write walls of text, every 2-3 lines need a visual break
- Use corporate jargon: leverage, unlock, game-changer, synergy, align, deep dive, revolutionize, paradigm, disrupt, empower, holistic, robust, thought leadership
- Put an external link in the body (40-60% reach penalty)
- Start AND end with a question, pick one
- Use more than 2 emojis per post
- Use a bare "Agree?" as a CTA (it invites a yes/no, not a real comment, and it is one of the weakest closes)
- **Bold, italic, or any markdown formatting in the post text**, LinkedIn plain text only, always. No exceptions. Bold unicode (𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱) is allowed in visuals/design but NEVER in the written post copy.

## @Tagging (recommended, not mandatory)

Tagging helps only when the tagged account is likely to engage. LinkedIn reads a tag that gets no response as a weak signal, so a tag on someone who ignores it, especially a large account that never likes back, can hurt more than it helps. Use it deliberately, not on every post.

**Tag when:**
- A tool genuinely appears in the workflow you describe (e.g. @Clay, @Instantly, @HubSpot, @n8n).
- A person is named in the post (e.g. a co-author).
- A partner or a friend who will actually like or comment, so the tag draws real early engagement.

**Keep it careful:**
- Only a handful of tags, never a wall. Over-tagging is penalized.
- Do not tag a big account that will not engage; a dead tag is a negative signal.
- Put each tag where the tool or person is first mentioned in the body, not dumped at the end.
- Never force-tag for reach, and never tag competitors.

## Sentence & Structural Rules

**Median line: 7 words. Hook lines: 9 words. Hard ceiling: 20 words.**
Sweet spot: 4-14 words per line. Most lines in strong posts fall here.

**Post length:**
- Target: 250-500 words
- Never under 100 words (short posts consistently underperform)
- 500+ words only for deep system reveals or case studies

**Paragraphs:** 1-2 sentences max, then whitespace break. No exceptions.

**Formatting hierarchy:**
1. Step 1: / Step 2: / Step 3:, for any process
2. ↳, for sub-points (preferred over bullets)
3. →, for action items or flow
4. Bold unicode, 1-3 key phrases, never more
5. Emojis: 0-2 max, used structurally only (👇 before CTA)

### USE these action verbs:

### NEVER USE:
leverage · unlock · game-changer · synergy · align · deep dive · revolutionize · paradigm · disrupt · empower · holistic · robust · thought leader · guru · ninja · visionary

### Power phrases (use these exact combinations: proven in top posts):
- "over to you", closer / CTA bridge
- "instead of", contrarian pivot
- "right now", urgency
- "at scale", capability framing
- "most people", pain opener
- "step by step", educational framing
- "here's how it works", reveal bridge
- "here's what we did", system reveal bridge
- "think of it as", analogy bridge
- "what this means for you", relevance pivot
- "what used to take", before/after framing
- "the result:", payoff reveal

## Numbers & Credibility Hierarchy

Always use a number. If you don't have your own, use the next best:

| Priority | Type | Example |
|----------|------|---------|
| 1 | Your own metric | "We've run this for [X]+ B2B clients" |
| 2 | Client result | "Generated 40 meetings in 6 weeks for a Series B SaaS client" |
| 3 | Campaign metric | "Reply rate went from 1.2% to 4.7% after switching the sequence" |
| 4 | Industry benchmark | "The average cold email reply rate in 2026 is 1.8%. We're hitting 4%+" |

**Company metrics and stack:** Always source these live, ask the user for the current numbers, client counts, or tool-stack details. Never hardcode a figure here, since those change and this file would drift.

## P.S.: Mandatory Structural Element

P.S. appears in the majority of top-performing posts. Every post ends with one of three P.S. types:

**Type 1, Resource offer (BoF):**
```
P.S. We built the Clay table template for this workflow.
Comment "ICP" and I'll send it over.
Make sure we're connected.
```

**Type 2, Service signal (pipeline):**
```
P.S. We build these systems for B2B GTM teams at Growth Today.
If you want us to set this up for you, DM me "GTM".
```

**Type 3, Follow CTA (ToF/MoF):**
```
P.S. I break down exactly how we build these systems every week.
Follow Growth Today if you want the next one.
```

**P.S. rules:**
- Always on its own line, separated from the body
- Never more than 3 lines
- Never put an external link here
- After P.S., the post is done, no more content

## Default Body Structure: The Step Framework

For any process, workflow, or system reveal:

```
[HOOK, bold result or claim, max 15 words]

[1-line credibility or context]

Here's [what we built / the exact system / how it works]:

Step 1: [Action label]
↳ [1-2 sentences of detail]
↳ [1-2 sentences of detail]

Step 2: [Action label]
↳ [1-2 sentences of detail]
↳ [1-2 sentences of detail]

Step 3: [Action label]
↳ [1-2 sentences of detail]

The result: [specific metric or outcome]

[CTA, question, comment-gate, or soft pointer]

P.S. [One of three P.S. types above]
```

## CTA Rules

CTA types, ranked by how many comments they typically drive:

| CTA Type | Relative comment volume | When to Use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Comment "[keyword]" gate | Highest (roughly 2.5x a no-CTA post) | BoF, only with a real deliverable. |
| Repost / save ask | High | ToF reach posts |
| Follow ask | Medium | ToF audience growth |
| No explicit CTA | Low to medium | Short MoF posts |
| Question / "over to you" | Lower | MoF discussion, pair with a P.S. |

**Comment-gate format:**
```
Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send it over.
Make sure we're connected so I can DM you.
```

## Timing

Quick reference only. Canonical source: the `scheduling` sub-skill and the timing note in `algorithm.md`. There is no reliable universal best day or hour. The old "Sunday advantage" and "06:00-07:00 UTC" claims were outlier artifacts in a small sample, not real effects. Post when your audience is online.

- Best in the US: 7-8 AM ET, Tuesday to Thursday.
- Weekends only if you have a large audience (20 to 30,000+ followers).
- Cadence: 3 to 4 posts per week, spread across ToF, MoF, and BoF. See the Weekly Plan above.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] First line is a result, bold claim, or number, NOT context
- [ ] Every major claim has a specific number
- [ ] Step 1/2/3 structure used for any process content
- [ ] ↳ arrows for sub-points (not dashes or bullets)
- [ ] Visual break every 2-3 lines, no wall of text
- [ ] 250-500 words target (never under 100)
- [ ] Post ends with P.S. (one of three types)
- [ ] One CTA only
- [ ] No external links in body or P.S.
- [ ] Max 2 emojis total
- [ ] No banned vocabulary (leverage, game-changer, etc.)
- [ ] Bold unicode used for 1-3 key phrases
- [ ] "We" for team posts, "I" for personal stories
- [ ] Visual recommended (image = 2.3x text-only reactions)
- [ ] Would I stop scrolling for this hook?
