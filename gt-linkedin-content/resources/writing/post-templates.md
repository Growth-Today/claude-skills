# LinkedIn Post Structure Templates

7 production-ready templates, each built from top-performing posts. Every one uses the Step / ↳ structure, a mandatory P.S., and the Growth Today voice. Copy the block, fill the brackets, ship.

---

## Template A: The System Reveal (1,800-2,500 chars)

**Best for:** Clay workflows, automation builds, campaign systems, AI agent reveals.
**Why it works:** step-by-step systems get saved and pull follow-up questions, so they earn a high comment-to-reaction ratio. A workflow visual roughly doubles reach.

```
[HOOK: Bold result + timeframe. Max 15 words.]

[1-line credibility: "We run this for [X]+ B2B GTM clients."]

Here's the exact system:

Step 1: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]
↳ [What happens next]

Step 2: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]
↳ [What happens next]

Step 3: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]
↳ [Result of this step]

[Optional Step 4-5 for complex systems]

The result: [Specific metric: meetings, leads, hours saved, reply rate]

[CTA: "Comment '[KEYWORD]' and I'll send the template." or "Over to you: what's your current [X] setup?"]

P.S. [Resource offer or service signal]
```

**Format:** dense single-image workflow or annotated screenshot, designed in your design tool.

## Template B: The Journey Post / How It Started (1,200-2,000 chars)

**Best for:** agency building, client wins, personal milestones, building in public.
**Why it works:** personal vulnerability plus specific numbers is our highest comment-quality format. People reply with their own stories instead of just reacting.

```
How it started: [Specific bad/humble state with number]
How it's going: [Specific impressive result with number]

[1-2 sentences of honest context: what changed]

Here's what made the difference:

1. [Lesson or milestone]
↳ [1-2 sentence explanation]

2. [Lesson or milestone]
↳ [1-2 sentence explanation]

3. [Lesson or milestone]
↳ [1-2 sentence explanation]

[Optional: 4-5 for longer posts]

[Closing sentence: forward-looking or grateful, not a CTA]

P.S. [Service signal or resource offer]
```

**Format:** text-only works here; add a proof visual if it's results-heavy.

## Template C: The Contrarian Take (1,000-1,800 chars)

**Best for:** hot takes, "X is dead", industry myths, thought leadership.
**Why it works:** a clear stance splits the room and drives debate in the comments, which is where our reach compounds. The body has to deliver the evidence the hook promises, never bait and disappoint.

```
[HOOK: "[Common belief] is [wrong/dead/overrated]." Max 10 words.]

[Acknowledge the common view: 1-2 sentences]

Here's what's actually happening:

↳ [Specific evidence from our experience, with number]
↳ [Second piece of evidence]
↳ [Third piece of evidence]

Here's what actually works instead:

↳ [Alternative approach 1]
↳ [Alternative approach 2]
↳ [Alternative approach 3]

[Proof point: "We've seen this across [X]+ B2B clients."]

Over to you: [Opinion question, NOT "Agree?"]

P.S. [Follow CTA or resource]
```

**Format:** a comparison visual ("how most people see X vs how GTM engineers see it"), Growth Today styling.

## Template D: The Resource Drop / Comment-Gate (800-1,500 chars)

**Best for:** lead magnets, template giveaways, trigger lists, playbook drops.
**Why it works:** comment-gating is the top comment driver, roughly 2.5x the comments of a no-CTA post. Only run it when you actually have the deliverable to send.

```
[HOOK: Bold claim + specific number. "37 of the best..." or "We've tested X and found..."]

[2-3 lines: why this matters to your reader right now]

Here's what's inside:

↳ [Teaser item 1: specific, not vague]
↳ [Teaser item 2]
↳ [Teaser item 3]
↳ [Bonus item or escalation]

Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send it over.
Make sure we're connected so I can DM you.

P.S. [Service signal: "We build these systems for B2B GTM teams at Growth Today. DM me 'GTM' to learn more."]
```

**Format:** clean list graphic, numbered items, brand colors.

## Template E: The Tool Comparison / Stack Reveal (1,500-2,500 chars)

**Best for:** tech stack reveals, head-to-head tool comparisons, "every tool we use".
**Why it works:** decisive verdicts are highly save-worthy, and readers tag colleagues to settle the debate. Be decisive and name a winner.

```
[HOOK: "I tested X [tools/platforms/approaches]." or "Every tool we use to run our [X] agency:"]

[1-line context: "Here's what we actually found after running this across [X]+ clients."]

[Tool/Category 1]:
↳ [What it does, 1 line]
↳ [Our verdict, specific, not vague]

[Tool/Category 2]:
↳ [What it does, 1 line]
↳ [Our verdict]

[Tool/Category 3]:
↳ [What it does, 1 line]
↳ [Our verdict]

[Continue for 4-7 tools depending on post type]

Overall winner for [use case]: [Pick one, be decisive]

Over to you: [question about their current stack]

P.S. [Follow CTA or resource offer: "We break down our full Clay + Claude stack every week. Follow Growth Today."]
```

**Format:** single dense tool-map image or a list graphic with tool logos, designed in your design tool.

## Template F: The Trend Explainer (1,000-1,800 chars)

**Best for:** AI/tech news with a GTM angle, new model releases, "what X means for your outbound".
**Why it works:** this is a reach play. Being early on a trend with a GTM translation pulls in new followers (target 1,000+ reactions). Keep the CTA soft, a resource CTA here hurts ToF distribution.

```
[HOOK: Trending topic name OR "Everyone's talking about [X]." OR "[X] just changed [Y]."]

[1-line promise: "Here's what it means for your GTM in 60 seconds."]

[Visual required: diagram, architecture chart, or explainer graphic]

What it is:
↳ [Simple 1-line definition]

Why it matters for GTM:
↳ [Specific GTM application 1]
↳ [Specific GTM application 2]
↳ [Specific GTM application 3]

What most people are missing:
↳ [The GTM-specific insight that's not obvious]

[Soft closer, no hard CTA for ToF posts]

P.S. [Follow CTA: "I translate AI trends into GTM plays every week. Follow Growth Today."]
```

**Format:** a visual is mandatory (diagram, infographic, or architecture image). Text-only ToF underperforms.

## Template G: The Case Study / Before-After (1,500-2,200 chars)

**Best for:** client results (anonymized), campaign breakdowns, ROI stories, proof posts.
**Why it works:** specific before-and-after numbers are the most credible proof on the platform. Result-led hooks (hard dollar figures, before to after) tend to cluster at the very top of performance. It moves a warm reader from "these people know their stuff" to "I want that result".

```
[HOOK: The headline result. "We took [client type] from [before number] to [after number] in [timeframe]."]

[1-line context: who they were and why they were stuck]

Before:
↳ [Problem 1 with a number]
↳ [Problem 2 with a number]

What we changed:

Step 1: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]

Step 2: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]

Step 3: [Action label]
↳ [Specific detail: tool, method, output]

After:
↳ [Result 1: before number to after number]
↳ [Result 2: before number to after number]

The principle: [One-line takeaway the reader can apply]

Over to you: [question about their current bottleneck]

P.S. [Service signal: "We build these systems for B2B GTM teams at Growth Today. DM me 'GTM'."]
```

**Format:** a before/after comparison visual or a metric card, designed in your design tool.

| Clay/automation workflow reveal | A | MoF | 1,800-2,500 chars |

| Agency milestone / client win | B | MoF/BoF | 1,200-2,000 chars |
| "How it started / how it's going" | B | MoF/BoF | 1,200-1,800 chars |
| Industry hot take / contrarian | C | MoF | 1,000-1,800 chars |
| "X is dead" / "Stop doing Y" | C | MoF | 1,000-1,500 chars |
| Lead magnet / template giveaway | D | BoF | 800-1,500 chars |
| Trigger list / playbook drop | D | BoF | 800-1,500 chars |
| Tech stack reveal | E | MoF | 1,500-2,500 chars |
| Tool comparison / head-to-head | E | MoF | 1,500-2,000 chars |
| AI/tech news + GTM angle | F | ToF | 1,000-1,800 chars |
| Trend explainer | F | ToF | 1,000-1,500 chars |
| Client result / ROI story | G | MoF/BoF | 1,500-2,200 chars |
| Campaign breakdown / before-after | G | MoF/BoF | 1,500-2,200 chars |
| Single image companion text | Any (shortened) | Any | 600-1,200 chars |
