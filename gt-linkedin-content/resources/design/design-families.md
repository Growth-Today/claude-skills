## Easily confused

- **Workflow vs Blueprint:** strict arrow sequence vs numbered phases readable in any order.
- **Comparison table vs 2x2 matrix:** discrete attribute columns vs a continuous two-axis plot.
- **Stage funnel vs Workflow:** narrowing/attrition vs equal-weight steps.
- **Flywheel vs Cycle:** compounding loop vs plain repeating loop.
- **Cheat sheet vs Numbered list:** dense multi-block canvas vs one idea per line.
- **Pyramid vs Funnel:** hierarchy of levels vs downward flow.
- **Venn vs 2x2 matrix:** circles with a labeled overlap are a Venn, even if captioned "matrix".

## Briefing a graphic

Once you have named the family, hand the graphic off with a fill-in-the-bracket brief. The templates (single graphic, carousel, motion graphic) and the cross-family build rules live in `design-briefs.md`.
