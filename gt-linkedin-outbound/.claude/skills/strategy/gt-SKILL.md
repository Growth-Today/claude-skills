## Strategy rules (full detail in the resources)

- The decay curve is normal: best-fit prospects respond in days 1-10, weeks 2-3 plateau, week 4+ is the fatigue zone.
- Fix the plateau structurally: micro-segmented lists, sender rotation, fresh angles, push non-responders to email. Never just "send more invites".
- Set expectations on the 90-day frame: month 1 is foundation and learning, month 2 is first consistent meeting flow, month 3 compounds. A campaign judged on day 14 gets killed before it can work.
