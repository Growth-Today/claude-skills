## Benchmark quick reference

| Metric | Baseline | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection acceptance rate | 15-20% | 25-35% | 40%+ |
| DM reply rate (post-accept) | 8-12% | 15-25% | 30%+ |
| Meeting rate (replies to meetings) | 10-15% | 20-30% | 35%+ |

Frame all figures as industry-reported ranges to validate against the account's own data, not as Growth Today-measured guarantees. Verify volatile numbers (limits, InMail rates) against official documentation before advising a client.
